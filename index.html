The weekend

Heat, tide, and a full stage.

Fire on the Island is a three-day music festival at the Amphitheater in Marathon, Florida, the first weekend of December. More than thirty bands share the grounds with vendors and entertainers — a Keys night built for people who like their tropics a little louder.
